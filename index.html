About CSMUN

Where Young Leaders Find Their Voice

CSMUN is a premier Model United Nations conference that empowers students to step into the shoes of world leaders. Through rigorous debate, collaborative negotiation, and thoughtful resolution-drafting, delegates develop critical thinking, public speaking, and leadership skills that last a lifetime.

Our Committees

Debate. Negotiate. Resolve.

Choose from our carefully curated committees, each tackling some of the world's most significant challenges.

UNGA

United Nations General Assembly

The chief deliberative, policymaking, and representative organ of the United Nations, addressing the most pressing global issues.

Agenda
"Addressing the Proliferation of Autonomous Weapons Systems"

UNHRC

United Nations Human Rights Council

Responsible for strengthening the promotion and protection of human rights around the globe and addressing situations of violations.

Agenda
"Safeguarding Digital Privacy in the Age of Mass Surveillance"
🌍

UNEP

United Nations Environment Programme

The leading global environmental authority that sets the environmental agenda and promotes sustainable development.

Agenda
"Financing Climate Adaptation in Developing Nations"
🛡️

UNSC

United Nations Security Council

The most powerful body of the UN, primarily responsible for the maintenance of international peace and security.

Agenda
"De-escalation of Geopolitical Tensions in the Indo-Pacific"
📊

ECOSOC

Economic and Social Council

Coordinates the economic and social work of the UN, promoting international economic cooperation and development.

Agenda
"Bridging the Global Digital Divide for Equitable Development"

Conference Schedule

Three Days of Intense Diplomacy

From opening ceremonies to the final gavel, every moment is crafted to challenge and inspire.

🎪
Day 1 — August 15

Opening Ceremony & Committee Session I

Keynote address, delegate orientation, and the first round of committee sessions. Set the tone for three transformative days.

🔥
Day 2 — August 16

Committee Sessions II & III

Deep-dive into agendas with moderated and unmoderated caucuses. Alliances form, working papers take shape, and debates intensify.

🏆
Day 3 — August 17

Final Session & Closing Ceremony

Draft resolutions go to vote. Award ceremony celebrates the best delegates, diplomats, and rising stars of CSMUN 2026.
